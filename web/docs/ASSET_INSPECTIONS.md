# Asset Inspections

Inspection, Preventive Maintenance, routine work, and corrective repair remain separate workflows:

- A **Routine Task** is a recurring operational duty such as cleaning, grass cutting, or garbage disposal.
- An **Inspection** assesses condition or safety and records observations. It may recommend preventive maintenance or corrective repair.
- **Preventive Maintenance** performs scheduled work intended to prevent failure, such as AC cleaning, filter service, lubrication, or pump servicing.
- A **Corrective Work Order** repairs a known defect and follows the normal Work Order workflow.

An inspection finding does not automatically become a Work Order. Inspectors can record **Preventive Maintenance Required** as an advisory recommendation without opening a repair request. A **Corrective Repair Required** finding may be sent through the existing asset review or Work Order process. Likewise, PM findings can explicitly create a linked corrective Work Order without marking the defect repaired.
